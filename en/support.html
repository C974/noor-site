Questions, bug reports or feedback about Noor? Write to support@usewyvo.com. Please mention your device model, operating system version and what you were doing. Please do not send personal information; Noor does not need any to help you.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Noor has no accounts and no sign-in. Open it and use it.

Does Noor work offline?

Partly. Quran text, tafseer and recitations are fetched from public services the first time you use them. From Settings → Offline content you can download the Quran text, tafseer and recitations for the reciters you choose, and then read and listen without a connection. Prayer times, the Qibla compass, the tasbih and your bookmarks work without internet.

How are prayer times calculated? Can they differ from my mosque?

Prayer times are calculated on your device from your location and the date. You can choose the calculation method (the Muslim World League is the default; the others are Umm al-Qura, Egyptian General Authority, ISNA, Karachi and the Moonsighting Committee) and the Asr convention (Shafi'i by default, or Hanafi). Times can differ by a few minutes from your local mosque's timetable, so please confirm with your local mosque.

I denied location permission. Can I still get prayer times?

Yes. In Prayer, choose a city from the list or search for one. Noor then calculates the times for it without needing location permission.

Prayer reminders do not arrive on time. What can I do?

Reminders are local notifications that you turn on in Prayer. Check that notifications are allowed for Noor in your device settings. Some phones restrict apps in the background or delay notifications to save battery; allow Noor to run without battery restrictions if reminders are late.

The Qibla compass looks wrong.

Compass accuracy depends on your phone's sensor. Move the phone in a figure-eight a few times to calibrate it, keep it flat and away from magnets, metal cases and electronics, and check that location permission is allowed. The bearing is calculated from your location, so make sure the place shown is correct.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device. In the app, Settings → Data lets you delete saved Quran text and tafseer, Settings → Offline content lets you delete downloaded recitations, and you can delete bookmarks and reset the tasbih. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in system settings, removes everything. The developer holds no data about you on any server.

How do I change the language or the numerals?

The app interface is Arabic. In Settings → Appearance → Numerals you can choose Arabic-Indic (٠١٢٣) or Western (0123) digits. The Quran translation and tafseer can be shown in English from the reader.

How do I back up my bookmarks and settings, or move to a new phone?

In Settings → Data, choose "Export a backup". It creates a single JSON file and opens your share sheet so you can save it wherever you like. On the new device, install Noor and choose "Import a backup". Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Where do the Quran, hadith and recitations come from?

See the credits inside the app (Settings → Sources and thanks) for the full list. The Quran text is shown unaltered. Please verify rulings and hadith gradings with a qualified scholar.

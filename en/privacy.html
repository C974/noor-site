Noor (نور) — Islamic Companion

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Noor is an Arabic-first Quran, prayer-times, hadith and remembrance app. It is built to collect as little as possible. Noor has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no server of its own. The developer does not receive, store or sell any personal data.

1. Summary

  • No account or sign-in. No advertising. No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Bookmarks, reading position, tasbih counts, settings, downloads and cached text stay on your device.
  • Your location is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never sent to the developer.
  • Typing a city name sends that text (only) to the Open-Meteo geocoding service so it can be turned into coordinates.
  • To show content, the app connects directly from your device to public services (listed in section 4). They see your IP address like any website would.
  • Prayer reminders are local notifications. There is no push server.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage (SQLite database, AsyncStorage and files):

  • Bookmarks, last-read position and tasbih counters.
  • Settings: theme, numerals, font size, reciter, translation, calculation method, reminder choices.
  • Your chosen or detected place (name and coordinates) used for prayer times.
  • Quran, tafseer and hadith text cached for offline reading.
  • Recitation audio files you chose to download.

None of this is uploaded by the developer. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage (see section 7).

4. Third-party services contacted by the app

The app makes direct HTTPS requests from your device to the public services below. Like any internet request, they inevitably receive your IP address and standard request metadata (such as your device's user agent), and they apply their own privacy practices. Noor does not control them and has no access to their logs. Noor sends no account, device identifier, advertising identifier or location coordinates to any of them.

Prayer times and Qibla do not use any online service. Tapping a source link (for example to sunnah.com or dorar.net from an adhkar source, or the credits links) opens your browser, which then follows that site's own privacy policy.

5. Backup, export and import

"Export a backup" creates one JSON file (bookmarks, reading position, tasbih, settings and saved place) and opens your system share sheet. Where it goes is entirely your choice. "Import a backup" lets you pick such a file from your device. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

6. Children

Noor contains no ads, no accounts, no user-generated content and no communication features, and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is suitable for all ages.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing on any server to access, correct or delete. Everything lives on your device and you control it:

  • In the app, More → Data: delete saved Quran text and tafseer. In More → Offline content: delete downloaded recitations.
  • Delete bookmarks and reset tasbih counts inside the app.
  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in system settings, to remove all of it.
  • Withdraw location or notification permission at any time in your device settings.

8. Your rights

Regardless of where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to know what is processed about you, and to access, correct and delete it. For Noor this is simple: no personal data is held by the developer. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. If you believe a request applies, write to us and we will answer.

9. Security

All network requests use HTTPS. Local data is protected by your device's operating-system sandbox and encryption. Backup files you export are not encrypted; keep them somewhere you trust.

10. International transfers

The third-party services above may process requests in countries other than your own.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address, and in the app when the change affects what the app does.
